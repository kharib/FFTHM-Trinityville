# FFTHM Trinityville Platform Vision

Version: 1.0
Owner: Fingers From The Heart Ministries - Trinityville
Current Steward: Khari Bryan

---

# Purpose

The FFTHM Trinityville Platform exists to create meaningful ministry engagement before, during, and after every interaction with the church.

The platform is not a website project.

The platform is a digital ministry ecosystem that uses multiple communication and engagement channels to connect with visitors, members, volunteers, ministry leaders, and pastoral leadership.

The website is one channel.

The Android application is another channel.

Future channels may include social media integrations, email communication, kiosks, smart devices, and AI-powered ministry tools.

---

# Mission

To remove barriers between people and ministry.

Whenever possible, church interactions should become:

- Easier
- Faster
- More accessible
- More engaging
- More measurable

---

# Engagement Philosophy

Every interaction is a ministry opportunity.

Examples:

## Before Service

Visitors can:

- Learn service times
- View upcoming events
- Watch previous messages
- Plan a visit
- Contact the church

## During Service

Members can:

- Access hymn references
- Open hymn resources
- View scripture references
- Access service notes
- Give electronically
- Submit prayer requests

## After Service

Members can:

- Register for events
- View announcements
- Watch recordings
- Receive notifications
- Follow ministry activities

# Service Companion Vision

One long-term objective is to provide a digital worship companion.

Examples:

- Hymn references
- Scripture references
- Service notes
- Offering information
- Prayer request submission

External resources such as Hymnal.net should be linked rather than duplicated.

---

# Giving Vision

The platform should facilitate:

- Tithes
- Offerings
- Missions
- Building funds
- Special causes

The platform should never process or store payment card information directly.

Trusted payment providers should handle payment processing.

---

# Design Principles

## API First

Any feature created for the website should be usable by:

- Android
- Future iOS
- Future integrations

without redesign.

---

## Mobile First

Assume the majority of users will interact through smartphones.

---

## Low Friction

Minimize steps required to complete ministry actions.

---

## Future Proof

The platform must remain adaptable to:

- Mobile applications
- AI assistants
- Automation
- Analytics
- Future cloud services

---

# Long-Term Goal

Transform FFTHM Trinityville into a digital ministry platform that supports engagement at every ministry touchpoint.
